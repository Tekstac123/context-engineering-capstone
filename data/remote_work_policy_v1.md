# Remote Work Policy (v1)

Employees may work remotely up to **2 days per week**, subject to manager approval.
Remote work requests must be submitted 5 business days in advance using the HR portal.
Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours (10 AM - 4 PM local time).

Equipment stipend: employees are eligible for a one-time home office stipend of **$300**.

Approval authority: direct manager only.
