# Remote Team Building & Offsite Events Policy

This policy covers remote team-building activities, virtual offsite
events, and quarterly remote social gatherings for distributed teams.

Teams working remotely are encouraged to schedule a monthly virtual
coffee chat. Budget for remote team events is $50 per employee per
quarter, coordinated through the People Experience team.

Remote employees joining an in-person offsite are eligible for travel
reimbursement per the Travel & Expense Policy.

Note: this policy governs team social events only and does not address
day-to-day remote work arrangements, which are covered separately.
