# Remote Work Policy (v2)

Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**, subject to manager approval.
Remote work requests must be submitted 3 business days in advance using the HR portal.
Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours (10 AM - 3 PM local time).

Equipment stipend: employees are eligible for a one-time home office stipend of **$500**.

Approval authority: direct manager, with department head sign-off for more than 3 days/week.

This version supersedes POL-RW-001 in its entirety, effective 2025-03-01.
